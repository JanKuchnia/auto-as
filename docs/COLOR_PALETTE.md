## 🎨 Palette Breakdown

### 🌌 Primary Dark Bases (Background & Containers)
* **Jet Black** (`#060709` / `rgb(6, 7, 9)`)
  * Deepest shadows, absolute blacks, and crisp background edges.
* **Dark Obsidian** (`#0D1117` / `rgb(13, 17, 23)`)
  * Main structural background canvas (ideal for body text layers).
* **Deep Navy Slate** (`#161C24` / `rgb(22, 28, 36)`)
  * Card background panels, bento-box borders, and elevated sections.

### 💎 Tech Accent Blues (Highlights & Branding)
* **Electric Cyber Blue** (`#0082FF` / `rgb(0, 130, 255)`)
  * Main branding elements, prominent titles, call-to-actions, and active states.
* **Deep Steel Blue** (`#0D4B81` / `rgb(13, 75, 129)`)
  * Subdued button fills, icon container backdrops, and soft ambient glows.
* **Muted Ice Blue** (`#5C93B4` / `rgb(92, 147, 180)`)
  * Informational subheaders, icon paths, and secondary graphics.

### 🧪 Product & Coating Packaging Accents
* **Graphene Dark Grey** (`#24272C` / `rgb(36, 39, 44)`)
  * Matte finish fills, structural borders, and inner input shadows.
* **Muted Sage Green** (`#527E6A` / `rgb(82, 126, 106)`)
  * Clean, tactical green accent used for protective/hydrophobic indicators.
* **Warm Copper Amber** (`#A3623B` / `rgb(163, 98, 59)`)
  * Premium heat or durability highlights, warning alerts, or specialized badges.

### 💬 Neutral Typography & Layout Contrast
* **Pure White** (`#FFFFFF` / `rgb(255, 255, 255)`)
  * Primary bold headlines, high-contrast labels, and status badges.
* **Light Platinum Grey** (`#D1D5DB` / `rgb(209, 213, 219)`)
  * Long-form paragraph descriptions, body text, and deactivated elements.
